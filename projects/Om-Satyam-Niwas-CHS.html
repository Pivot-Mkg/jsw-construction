Om Satyam Niwas CHS, Borivali, Mumbai

A signature JSK Buildwell residence crafted with timeless architecture, premium materials, and a fully custom interior experience.

Om Satyam Niwas CHS

Om Satyam Niwas CHS is a redevelopment project in Borivali, Mumbai, the project features a mixeduse development with retail spaces at ground level, two floors of podium parking, and residential apartments from the 3rd to the 15th floor. It comprises 63 homes-including 45 thoughtfully planned 2 BHK apartments and 18 spacious 3 BHK residences- along with 18 retail shops.

This redevelopment replaced an original society of 18 residential members and six shop owners, previously a ground-plus-two-storey structure built in 1975. The agreement was signed in January 2018, and the project was completed in June 2023 over a span of 66 months. The development is fully delivered, with the Occupation Certificate received in July 2023.

  • Ground floor shopping
  • 2 BHK & 3 BHK Flats
  • 1st and 2nd-floor podium parking
  • 3rd to 15th floors residential
