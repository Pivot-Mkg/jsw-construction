Siddeshwar Gardens, Kolshet Road, Thane

A signature JSK Buildwell residence crafted with timeless architecture, premium materials, and a fully custom interior experience.

Siddeshwar Gardens

Siddeshwar Gardens is a 5 lakh sq. ft. integrated township on Kolshet Road one of Thane’s fastest-appreciating residential corridors. Developed in two phases, the project includes row houses, mid-rise towers, and high-rise towers ranging from 15 to 27 storeys.

The development is fully occupied, with property values appreciating approximately 12% annually since completion. Residents include a mix of families, working professionals, and retirees drawn by the project’s combination of space, amenities, and connectivity.

Project Highlights

  • Total Area - 5,00,000 sq. ft. (built-up)
  • Location - Kolshet Road, Thane West
  • Development Period - Phase 1: 2006-2010 | Phase 2: 2011-2015
  • Total Units - 8 Row Houses + 412 Apartments
  • Configuration - 1.5 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK apartments
  • Status - OC Recived

Harmony

15-storey 2 & 3 BHK tower with Yeoor Hill views.

Phase I | Delivered

Part of Phase I of Siddeshwar Gardens, this 15-storeyed residential tower spans a total built-up area of 1,10,000 sq. ft. and was completed in under two years. The development offers well-planned 2 and 3 BHK homes designed for comfortable urban living while being surrounded by natural serenity.

From the 8th floor onwards, residents enjoy uninterrupted views of the Yeoor Hills, creating a rare blend of city convenience and scenic tranquility. With the Occupation Certificate in place and thoughtfully curated lifestyle amenities, the project delivers a balanced living environment for families.

Project Highlights

  • Total built-up area of 1,10,000 sq. ft.
  • 15-storeyed tower with spacious 2 & 3 BHK homes.
  • Completed in under two years (Phase I – Siddeshwar Gardens)
  • Uninterrupted Yeoor Hills views from the 8th floor onwards
  • Occupation Certificate received
  • Landscaped gardens, playground, tennis court, and jogging track

Riviera

20-storey luxury tower on Kolshet Road.

Phase II | Delivered

Rising to 20 storeys with a configuration of stilt plus 18 floors, this residential development spans a total built-up area of 1,30,000 sq. ft. and forms Phase II of Siddeshwar Gardens. Construction began in January 2015 and was completed in 38 months by March 2018, reflecting steady execution and timely delivery.

The project offers a well-considered mix of two-bedroom homes, standard three-bedroom apartments, and extended three-bedroom residences featuring a dedicated utility room. With the Occupation Certificate received, the development stands as a thoughtfully designed living space tailored for practical comfort and modern family needs.

Project Highlights

  • Total built-up area of 1,30,000 sq. ft.
  • 20-storeyed tower (stilt + 18 floors)
  • Mix of 2 BHK, 3 BHK, and extended 3 BHK with utility room
  • Extended 3 BHK with dedicated utility room

Jasmine

19-storey tower with 1.5 & 2 BHK homes.

Phase II | Delivered

Rising on Kolshet Road one of Thane’s fastest-growing corridors Jasmine is a 19-storey address that blends smart design with everyday luxury. Offering well-planned 1.5 BHK and 2 BHK homes, it forms a key part of Phase II of Siddheshwar Gardens.

Delivered on schedule, Jasmine stands today as a ready, refined residence—crafted for modern urban living.

Project Highlights

  • A 19-storey luxurious tower.
  • Offers 1.5 BHK and 2 BHK apartments.
  • Status: Occupation Certificate received.
