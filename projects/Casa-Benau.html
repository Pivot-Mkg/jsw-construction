CASA BENAU 94 & CASA BENAU 95, South Goa

CASA BENAU 94 & CASA BENAU 95, South Goa is a private villa in Benaulim, South Goa.

JSK Homes brings you closer to the life you have worked and waited for. Own a private villa in the heart of Benaulim, South Goa, where your days unfold between your own pool, lush green gardens, and a sun-warmed terrace.

South Goa offers a way of living shaped by calm and quiet—far removed from the rush of everyday life. Here, serenity settles in naturally, inviting a slower rhythm that restores balance to both mind and soul.

Designed for discerning buyers, Casa Benau is set in one of South Goa’s most sought-after villages. Just minutes from Margao yet away from tourist congestion, Benaulim blends everyday convenience with lasting value—driven by limited land availability and strong demand from Mumbai, Bangalore, and NRI buyers.

The Villas

Casa Benau comprises two standalone villas – Villa 94 and Villa 95 – built on adjoining plots. Each villa is identical in layout and specifications, designed to maximize privacy, natural light, and indoor–outdoor living that defines the Goan lifestyle.

Why South Goa?

Limited Supply
South Goa combines tranquility, natural beauty, and exclusivity, attracting HNIs seeking privacy and refined living. Limited development and low density make it a scarce market with strong appreciation potential.
Infrastructure Upgrade
The new Mopa Airport improves access to South Goa, spurring interest in the region.
Remote Work Shift
Post-COVID demand surge from professionals seeking work-from-anywhere homes.
Rental Yields
Premium villas in South Goa command ₹25,000–50,000 per night during peak season (November–February).
Capital Appreciation
South Goa villa prices have increased 40–60% over the past 5 years.
Clean Ownership Framework
South Goa projects with clear land titles, local panchayat approvals, and transparent ownership structures offer peace of mind alongside long-term value.
